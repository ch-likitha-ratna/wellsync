Code of Conduct

Workplace Behavior & Ethics

In the workplace area, please be respectful of others and maintain a moderate tone to not distract others' work.

Discrimination/Harassment

There are zero-tolerance policies for any form of harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior. If such behavior is reported, it will result in the highest ticket severity. This will be reported to the Managers and higher up the chain.

Confidentiality

All employees are expected to maintain the confidentiality of sensitive information. Sharing confidential information without proper authorization is strictly prohibited.

Resources

You are welcome to use the company resources, such as the conference rooms, desktops/monitors, stationary materials, and beverages. Pleasure be sure to organize the ares which you have used in the same way you found it.

Other

Please be sure to attend meetings (whether in-person or online) on time.

Dress appropriately - formal wear is not necessary, but something that is comfortable for you and is in business casual is preferred.
