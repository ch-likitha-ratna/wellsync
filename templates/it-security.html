IT & Security

Acceptable Use of Devices and Software

All company-provided devices and software must be used solely for work-related purposes.

Employees are expected to:

  • Use approved software and applications only.
  • Avoid installing unlicensed or unauthorized tools.
  • Refrain from accessing inappropriate or harmful websites.
  • Ensure their devices are used in a secure, ethical, and respectful manner.

Password & Authentication Rules

To protect company systems and data, the following rules must be followed:

  • Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include letters, numbers, and symbols/
  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) must be enabled on all supported accounts.
  • Do not share passwords with anyone!

Data Privacy & Handling Confidential Information

Protecting client and company data is a top priority. Employees must:

  • Never share confidential data over unsecured channels.
  • Only access data necessary for your role.
  • Use encrypted storage and secure sharing methods for sensitive documents.
  • Follow GDPR, HIPAA, and other applicable data regulations if handling user data.

VPN, Device Encryption & Remote Access

To support hybrid and remote work securely:

  • Employees must connect to the company VPN (Virus Protected Network) when working outside the office.
  • All company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled.
  • Remote desktop or SSH access must be configured with approval from the IT team.
  • Avoid using public Wi-Fi without VPN protection.
  • Lock or shut down devices when unattended.
